Forage Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Forage (me.dougals.forage) is a free, open-source RSS reader for Android, published by Dougie Richardson ("I", "me"). This policy explains what the app does with your information.

In short

I do not collect, receive, store or share any of your data. The app has no accounts with me, no analytics, no advertising and no crash reporting. Everything the app keeps stays on your device, and the only network requests it makes are the ones needed to read the feeds you choose.

What the app stores on your device

  • Your subscriptions and reading data: the feeds, folders and articles you subscribe to, along with which articles you have read or saved, in a local database.
  • Offline copies: article text and images may be saved on your device so you can read them without a connection.
  • Settings: your preferences, such as theme, font, language and refresh schedule.
  • Nextcloud credentials (optional): if you connect a Nextcloud News server, the server address and an app password issued by your server are stored on your device in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot read and which Android encrypts at rest.

This data stays on your device. App backup is disabled, so it is not copied to Google Drive or other backups by the app. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it.

Network connections the app makes

The app uses the internet permission only to do what you ask:

  • Feeds and articles: it downloads the feeds you subscribe to, and the articles, thumbnails and images they link to, directly from those websites. Those sites can see your IP address and standard request details (such as the app's user agent), as with any web browser or feed reader. Forage does not send these requests through any server of mine.
  • Nextcloud News (optional): if you enable it, the app talks directly to the Nextcloud server you specify to sync feeds and read states. That server is run by you or your provider, and its privacy practices are their own.
  • Opening links: when you open an article in your browser, the browser and the website it opens handle that visit under their own policies.

The suggested feeds shown in the app are bundled with it, so browsing them makes no network requests until you subscribe.

Files you choose

If you import or export an OPML file, the app reads or writes only the file you pick through Android's file picker. It does not scan your storage.

Permissions

  • INTERNET: to download feeds, articles and images, and to sync with Nextcloud if you enable it.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: to check whether you are online or on a metered connection, so background refreshes can respect your settings.

The app does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or other personal information.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking code, and I do not sell or share data, because I do not have any. The app is distributed through Google Play, and Google may process information about your installation and use of Play under its own policy:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • Google Play privacy, security and deception policies
  • Google Play Data safety information

Children

Forage is a general-purpose reader and is not directed at children. I do not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children.

Your choices

Because the data lives on your device, you control it. You can remove feeds, delete saved articles, disconnect Nextcloud in the app's settings, or delete everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it. To remove data held by a Nextcloud server, use that server.

Open source

Forage is free software under the GPL-3.0 licence. You can inspect exactly what it does at github.com/DougieWougie/forage.

Changes to this policy

If the app's behaviour changes in a way that affects privacy, I will update this page and the date above before the change is released.
